No testnet wallet yet?

This runs on the Zcash testnet: its coins (TAZ) are free and worth nothing. About five minutes:

  1. Install Zingo! and choose testnet when you create a wallet.
  2. Copy your testnet receiving address. The faucet wants the form that starts with utest1 (not zu… or tu…).
  3. Get free TAZ at faucet.testnet.valargroup.dev: 0.125 TAZ a day is enough for many offerings (each costs 0.00001 TAZ plus the network fee).
  4. Come back, write your secret, and scan the QR with Zingo!.

Before you give it anything, read this.

private the link between you and your secret. We, the operator, cannot learn who contributed.

public the content of your secret, to everyone who holds the key, forever. The key cannot be revoked.

not guaranteed that nobody recognises your words. Content can identify you; names and places identify you.

Your own wallet stores what you sent. Its server sees your IP address and when you sent: our Tor does not cover your phone, and Tor does not hide timing. If you add a reply address, anyone holding the key can see it and write to it. Order inside a block is miner order, not send order.
