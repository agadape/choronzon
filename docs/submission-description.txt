WHAT IT DOES
A collective god made of secrets. You give it a secret as a Zcash shielded payment (fixed amount, text in the encrypted memo). Its full viewing key is published, so anyone can rebuild the work from the chain: each offering becomes an eye on its body and a line spoken by one synthetic voice, and every machine with the key gets the same score_hash and render_hash. Its own lines count only when its wallet pays itself; copies sent by anyone else are refused. An offering may carry a shielded reply address; a listener can whisper back straight to it.

WHY IT DOES NOT LEAK THE SENDER
It publishes what was said and hides who paid: the key shows every memo, never the payer. That comes from the shielded protocol, not from a promise of ours. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud AI. The kiosk sends from a throwaway wallet and reaches lightwalletd over Tor, failing closed. The public page builds the payment request in your browser and makes no third-party request.
Limits we state: your secret is public to every key holder, forever; your words can identify you; your wallet keeps what you sent; your phone wallet's server sees your IP and, the key being public, can read that offering; timing can link (the kiosk room, our web host's logs); reply addresses must be shielded-only.

WHAT IS REAL
On Zcash testnet (NU7, Ironwood), each with a txid in docs/evidence-index.md: kiosk offerings heard 1-4 min after sending; Whisper Back end to end; the god's voice accepted and a forgery refused; a phone offering from the public page via Zingo!; a GitHub runner that, from the key alone, printed the same render_hash. Key at choronzon.vercel.app/key.txt.

WHAT IS NOT
Testnet only (Ironwood is NU7). Nearly all offerings are the developer's labelled seeds and tests; no stranger has used it yet. The site is a snapshot rebuilt ~every 30 min. Whisper Back works at the installation or by CLI, not on the public page.
