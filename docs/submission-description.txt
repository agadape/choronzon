WHAT IT DOES
A collective god made of secrets. You give it a secret as a Zcash shielded transaction (fixed amount, text in the memo). Its viewing key is public, so anyone can rebuild the whole work from the chain: every offering becomes an eye on its body and a line spoken by one pinned ghost voice. Two machines with the key get the same score_hash and render_hash. Strangers can whisper back to an offering's one-time reply address, shielded and direct. Its own lines count only when its wallet pays itself; forgeries are refused.

WHY IT DOES NOT LEAK THE SENDER
The content is public to every key holder, forever; only the link between sender and secret is private. Offerings are shielded payments, so the key shows what was said, never who paid. The protocol needs no name or account; no analytics, no cloud AI (voice, mix and render are local). Our kiosk sends from an ephemeral wallet, overwritten and deleted after use, and our kiosk and renderer reach lightwalletd over Tor, failing closed. The public page builds the payment request in your browser and sends nothing (strict CSP, no referrer). Published renders are anchored in Bitcoin (OpenTimestamps): we cannot rewrite them silently.
Stated limits: your own wallet keeps what you sent; words and style can identify you; Tor hides IP, not timing; a phone wallet uses its own server; a kiosk reply address belongs to the kiosk's session wallet, which the operator can read.

WHAT IS REAL
All of it runs on Zcash testnet (NU7, Ironwood), with txids in docs/evidence-index.md: kiosk offerings, whisper back, the god's voice vs a forgery, a phone offering from the public page via Zingo! (efc5ef44…), a GitHub runner reproducing the hash from the key alone. Key in the README and at choronzon.vercel.app/key.txt.

WHAT IS NOT
Testnet only (Ironwood is NU7). Most offerings so far are the team's labelled seeds and tests. The site is a snapshot, rebuilt ~every 30 min. Whisper Back runs at the installation or by CLI, not on the public page.
